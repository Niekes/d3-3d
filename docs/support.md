# Support d3-3d

**d3-3d** is free, and it always will be. There is no company behind it — just one maintainer working in spare time — so the cost of keeping it alive is paid for by the people who depend on it.

If d3-3d saves you time, [become a sponsor](https://github.com/sponsors/niekes).

## What sponsorship buys

| Tier        | What you get                                                                                            |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **$5/mo**   | Your name in the [sponsors list](https://github.com/Niekes/d3-3d#sponsors) and my sincere thanks       |
| **$25/mo**  | The above, plus your logo in the README and on this site, and priority issue triage                    |
| **$100/mo** | The above, plus direct email access for feature requests and a mention in the release notes            |

## Where the money goes

- **Issue triage and bug fixes.** Sponsorship buys a reply within a couple of days instead of whenever I get to it.
- **Types that stay correct.** Keeping `index.d.ts` accurate as d3, TypeScript and the DOM move on.
- **The documentation site.** Hosting, builds and the example gallery.
- **Time for new work.** More shapes, better performance, and eventually a WebGL renderer.

Prefer a one-off? The [sponsor page](https://github.com/sponsors/niekes) also takes one-time contributions.

## Company sponsorship

Need an invoice, a purchase order, or sponsorship under your employer's name? [Get in touch](mailto:s@niekes.com).

## Other ways to help

Sponsorship is not the only way to keep d3-3d alive, and it is not required to use the library:

- **Star the repo** — it is the cheapest signal that helps the project get found.
- **Report bugs well.** A reproducible issue is worth more than a star.
- **Answer a question** in [Discussions](https://github.com/Niekes/d3-3d/discussions) or in someone else's issue thread.
- **Contribute code.** See [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/Niekes/d3-3d/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md).
